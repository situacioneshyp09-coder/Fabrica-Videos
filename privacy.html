Privacy Policy

Fabrica Videos · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Fabrica Videos ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by Russell Quiroz to upload videos that I create to my own social media accounts, including TikTok. The app is not offered to the public.

Information the app accesses. When I connect my TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and open ID) for my own account only, as permitted by the scopes I approve (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How the information is used. The information is used solely to upload and publish videos, with their captions, to my own TikTok account, and to confirm which account is connected.

Storage. Access tokens are stored locally on my own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, and no database or analytics service is used.

Sharing. The app does not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok through TikTok's official APIs in order to publish content.

Retention and deletion. Tokens are kept only while the account remains connected. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions), and the locally stored token can be deleted at any time.

Children. The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above will reflect the latest version.
